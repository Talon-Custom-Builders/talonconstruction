Welcome to The Journal

September 8, 2026 Around Nevada County

Most of what we know about building in Nevada County was learned on job sites, not in a classroom. This is where we are going to start writing some of it down.

Homeowners ask us the same questions before a project starts. What will this actually cost? How long will it take? Why does that slope change the foundation? Do I need a permit for this? What happens if we find something once the walls are open? Those are good questions, and they deserve better than a rushed answer over the phone.

The Journal is where we will answer them properly, one at a time.

We expect to write about three things. The realities of building in the foothills — slopes, access, drainage, lake-area requirements, aging structures, and the weather that shapes all of it. Planning and budget, which is the part of a project most likely to go wrong and the easiest to get right early. And the work itself: what we are building, what we ran into, and how it was solved.

We are not going to write posts that say nothing. If we do not have something useful to say about a subject, we will wait until we do.

For anyone who has not worked with us: Talon Construction Company builds custom homes, major remodels, additions, decks, docks, and outdoor living spaces in Penn Valley, Lake Wildwood, and throughout Nevada County. Michael Novo has personally held California general contractor's license CSLB #397350 since 1981, and that license stands behind every job we take.

Talon is more than one person now. Erik Oxenbol and Jordon O'Hara have been with the company long enough to bring their own construction experience to it — Erik on management, and how a project gets organized and run from start to finish; Jordon in the field, on building knowledge and day-to-day leadership on site. They work to the standards Mike set. You will see all three names in what gets written here.

The standard we try to hold to here is the same one we bring to a job. Say what is true, explain the decisions, and do not promise what cannot be delivered.

If there is something you would like us to write about — a question you have not been able to get a straight answer to — call us at (530) 432-3633 or send it through the contact form. That is a genuine offer. The best posts here will come from questions homeowners actually asked.
